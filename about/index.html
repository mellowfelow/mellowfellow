Our Story

The Mellow Difference

Mellow Fellow was founded in 2019 in South Florida by a PhD chemist, a licensed pharmacist, and cannabis industry entrepreneurs who shared a single conviction: hemp-derived products should be formulated with the same rigor as pharmaceuticals. That science-first philosophy shaped everything that followed.

From 2019 to 2021, the founding team spent two full years on research and formulation before bringing a single product to market. While other brands rushed to launch around individual cannabinoids, Mellow Fellow developed a proprietary approach to mood-specific cannabinoid blending — combining multiple cannabinoids, terpenes, and delivery methods to target a specific experience rather than a single compound. The result was the Destination Series: curated blends like Euphoria, Creativity, Dream, Tranquility, and Desire, each engineered for a distinct effect.

Milestones

From Lab Bench to National Brand

2019 — Founded in South Florida. A PhD chemist and a licensed pharmacist join forces with cannabis industry entrepreneurs. The team begins two years of formulation research focused on mood-specific cannabinoid blending.

2019–2021 — Science-first development. The founding team develops and refines proprietary blends combining THCp, THCh, delta-8, delta-9, and natural terpenes. Every formulation is lab-tested through multiple iterations before reaching consumers. The Destination Series concept takes shape — products designed around a target experience, not a single molecule.

2022 — M-Fusions launch and rare cannabinoid expansion. Mellow Fellow introduces the M-Fusions edibles line, featuring live resin infusion at 1000mg potency. The brand simultaneously expands into rare cannabinoids including THCp disposables, THCh vapes, and THCa flower, becoming one of the first brands to offer the full spectrum of legal hemp-derived cannabinoids.

2023–Present — Retail partnerships and category growth. The product line grows to over 250 SKUs spanning seven categories: disposable vapes, vape cartridges, edibles, THCa flower, concentrates, syringes, and THC drinks. Mellow Fellow ships to all 50 states from South Florida, with a minimum order of $100 and free shipping on orders over $200.

Partnerships

Who We Work With

Mellow Fellow does not operate in isolation. The brand maintains active partnerships with organizations that strengthen its formulation science, regulatory compliance, and community impact:

Arvida Labs — Mellow Fellow's formulation partner. Arvida Labs provides the analytical chemistry and product development infrastructure behind every blend in the Destination Series. Their ISO-accredited laboratory handles potency testing, terpene profiling, and stability analysis for every batch.

Florida Healthy Alternatives Association — An industry compliance organization focused on ensuring hemp-derived products meet state and federal regulatory standards. Mellow Fellow works with FHAA to stay ahead of evolving Farm Bill compliance requirements.

Good Fellows Coalition — A community advocacy initiative that Mellow Fellow co-founded to promote responsible cannabinoid education, harm reduction, and equitable access to hemp-derived wellness products.

Quality

What Sets Us Apart

Every Mellow Fellow product is Farm Bill compliant, hemp-derived, and third-party lab tested by ISO-accredited laboratories. COAs (Certificates of Analysis) are available on request for any product. The brand uses no cutting agents, no synthetic additives, and no artificial cannabinoids.

The Destination Series alone spans 18 mood-specific blends — Euphoria, Dream, Creativity, Introvert, Tranquility, Desire, Charged, Recover, Motivation, Wanderlust, Clarity, Laughter, Grounded, Connection, Adventure, Harmony, Limitless, and Passion — each with a distinct cannabinoid and terpene profile calibrated by the founding team.

Whether you are exploring cannabinoids for the first time or looking for a brand that takes formulation as seriously as you take what you put in your body, Mellow Fellow was built for you.
