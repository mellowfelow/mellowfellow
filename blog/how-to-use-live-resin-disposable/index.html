How to Use a Mellow Fellow Live Resin Disposable

How to Use a Mellow Fellow Live Resin Disposable Vape

Mellow Fellow disposables are designed to work straight out of the box — but a few techniques make a significant difference in flavor quality, vapor production, and how long your device lasts. This guide covers everything from first draw to last puff.

Step 1: Prime Your Device Before First Use

Before your first draw, take 2-3 short gentle puffs without inhaling. This warms the coil and allows the live resin oil to saturate the wick evenly. Skipping this step — especially in cold weather — can produce a harsh or burnt-tasting first hit. Priming draws take about 2 seconds each. You will notice vapor quality improve noticeably from your first real draw after priming.

Step 2: Draw Technique for Maximum Flavor

Long slow draws of 3-5 seconds produce better vapor quality than short sharp pulls. Live resin requires slightly lower heat than distillate to preserve the terpene profile. A longer, gentler pull gives the coil time to vaporize at optimal temperature. Take the vapor into your mouth first, then inhale to the lungs. This mouth-to-lung technique maximizes terpene flavor and reduces throat harshness.

Charging Your Device

All Mellow Fellow disposables are USB-C rechargeable. The LED indicator flashes when battery is low. Connect to any USB-C cable and charge for 20-30 minutes. Do not leave charging unattended overnight. The 4mL Duo device may need 2-3 charge cycles over its lifespan given the larger oil volume.

Storage to Preserve Flavor

Store your disposable upright at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Heat degrades terpenes rapidly — a car glove box in summer is one of the worst storage locations. Cold temperatures temporarily thicken the oil and reduce flow, but the device performs normally once warmed to room temperature. Stored correctly, a Mellow Fellow disposable maintains full flavor quality for 6-12 months.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Weak vapor: check battery, warm device in hands 60 seconds, try a slower longer draw. Burnt taste: device is likely empty. Gurgling: store vertically for 10 minutes before drawing.
