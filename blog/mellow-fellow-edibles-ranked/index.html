Mellow Fellow Edibles Ranked: All Products Tested & Compared 2026

Mellow Fellow has one of the most diverse edible lineups in the hemp space — ranging from gentle Microdose gummies designed for newcomers all the way to the 10,000mg Max Dose line built for heavy consumers. This guide ranks every edible category by potency, explains what each one does, and helps you find the right product for your experience level.

The Full Mellow Fellow Edibles Lineup

Here is the complete range, organized from mildest to most intense:

1. Wellness Gummies — Mildest, Non-Intoxicating

The Wellness line is the one edible category from Mellow Fellow that does not aim to get you high. These are CBG and CBD-focused gummies designed for calm, balance, and general wellbeing. The Happy CBG gummies come in 100mg and 1,500mg total options. The Relief CBD gummies are watermelon-flavored and similarly focused on physical comfort. If you are looking for a functional daily supplement rather than a recreational product, start here.

2. Microdose Gummies — Gentle Introduction

The Microdose line comes in at 80mg total per package across multiple flavors including watermelon, blue razz, cherry, and peach. These are low-dose Delta-9 products — 80mg total spread across multiple pieces means each individual gummy is a modest serving. A good starting point for anyone who wants a mild edible experience without committing to a full-potency product.

3. Delta-8 Edibles — Smooth and Relaxing

The Delta-8 edible line delivers a milder, more relaxed experience compared to Delta-9. Options include the D8 Dream Live Resin in Green Apple (1,000mg) and the Delta-8 Euphoria Live Resin in Peach (1,000mg). These are for experienced users who are comfortable with edibles but want a gentler ride than full Delta-9 or THCp.

4. Delta-9 Gummies — Classic, Well-Rounded

The Delta-9 gummies are where most experienced hemp consumers land. These deliver a classic THC edible experience with full-spectrum Delta-9 at compliant dose levels. The Seltzer and Drink formats also fall into the Delta-9 category — the Strawberry Mango Delta-9 Seltzer (20mg, 4-pack) and the Delta-9 Drink Mixers are popular for social situations where a standard edible would be too slow or cumbersome.

5. Baklava Sticks — Unique Format, 40mg Each

The Baklava Sticks are one of the more creative products in the Mellow Fellow catalog. At 40mg per piece in Cookies & Cream and Delta-9 flavors, these are a notch above Microdose and well above a beginner threshold. The format is unusual and makes them a good conversation piece — but the 40mg dose is real and should be respected.

6. Live Resin Edibles — Full-Spectrum Experience

The Live Resin edibles bring the quality of live resin extraction into gummy form. The Live Resin Watermelon Rings (Delta-9) and the 400mg Live Resin gummies in Mamey Mango Coconut are standouts. Live resin preserves a fuller terpene profile than standard distillate edibles, which many users describe as producing a more nuanced, less one-dimensional effect. For more on why this matters, see our live resin vs distillate breakdown.

7. Blends Edibles — Euphoria Formula

The Blends Edibles — including the Euphoria blend Watermelon at 1,000mg — use a cannabinoid blend formula designed to produce a specific effect profile. These are for experienced consumers who want a targeted experience rather than a generic THC effect.

8. THCp Bursts — Most Potent Per Piece

The THCp Bursts come in at 25mg per piece in flavors including Strawberry Cheesecake, Key Lime Pie, and Caramel Apple Pie. THCp is estimated to be up to 33x more potent than Delta-9 at the receptor level, which means 25mg of THCp is not the same as 25mg of a standard Delta-9 gummy — not even close. These are for very experienced consumers only. If you have not tried Mellow Fellow edibles before, do not start here.

9. Max Dose — Strongest Total Content

The Max Dose edibles at 10,000mg per package are the most extreme product in the lineup. Available in Watermelon flavor. These are not a general recommendation — they exist for high-tolerance consumers who need significant doses to achieve their desired effect. New users should stay well clear.

Which Mellow Fellow Edible Is Right for You?

If you are new: Wellness gummies or Microdose. If you are experienced with edibles: Delta-9 gummies, Live Resin edibles, or Delta-8 options. If you want the most potent experience available and have significant tolerance: THCp Bursts or Max Dose. When in doubt, go lower and be patient with onset time — edibles take 30–90 minutes to fully kick in. Redosing too early is the most common mistake with edibles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What edibles does Mellow Fellow make?

Mellow Fellow's edible lineup includes THCp Bursts, Max Dose, Microdose gummies, Delta-9 gummies, Live Resin edibles, Baklava Sticks, Delta-8 edibles, Wellness gummies, and Blends Edibles. Browse the full edibles collection.

What is the strongest Mellow Fellow edible?

Max Dose at 10,000mg total is the strongest by volume. THCp Bursts at 25mg per piece are the most potent per-piece option given THCp's extremely high CB1 binding affinity.

How long do Mellow Fellow edibles take to kick in?

30–90 minutes is typical. Full effect may not peak until 2 hours after consumption. Do not redose within the first 90 minutes.

Are Mellow Fellow Delta-9 gummies legal?

Yes. All Mellow Fellow Delta-9 gummies are hemp-derived and farm-bill compliant, containing no more than 0.3% Delta-9 THC by dry weight. They are federally legal and ship to most US states.

What are Mellow Fellow Baklava Sticks?

Baklava Sticks are a Delta-9 THC edible product with 40mg per serving, available in Cookies & Cream flavor. They are an unusual format in the hemp edibles market and sit in the moderate-to-strong potency range.
