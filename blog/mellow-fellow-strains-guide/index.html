Mellow Fellow Strains Guide: Indica vs Sativa vs Hybrid Explained

Mellow Fellow products are available in a wide range of strain options across vapes, cartridges, and flower — and choosing the right strain makes a meaningful difference to your experience. This guide covers every major strain in the Mellow Fellow lineup, what to expect from each one, and how to match strains to your intended effect.

Why Strain Choice Matters with Mellow Fellow

Strain selection matters most in products where terpenes are preserved — specifically live resin and terp sauce products. In these formats, the original plant's terpene profile survives the extraction process and continues to shape the effect. In distillate-based products, strain names still influence the added terpene blend, though the effect differentiation is subtler. Either way, choosing a strain that matches your desired outcome (energy vs calm, social vs solo, daytime vs nighttime) gives you a better experience than picking at random.

Sativa Strains in the Mellow Fellow Lineup

Green Crack — Available in the Euphoria Blend 2ml Live Resin Disposable. A classic energizing sativa known for sharp mental focus and mood elevation. Good for daytime use, creative tasks, or social situations. One of the most popular sativa options in the lineup.

Sour Diesel — Featured in the Creativity Blend 4ml Live Resin Disposable. Sour Diesel is a fuel-forward, cerebral sativa that pairs naturally with the Creativity blend's energizing cannabinoid formula. A strong choice for productive or creative sessions.

Jack Herer — Available in the Clarity Live Resin 2ml Cart. Jack Herer is a legendary sativa known for clear-headed stimulation without overwhelming intensity. The Clarity cart is a reliable daytime option for experienced sativa users.

Strawberry Cough — Appears across the Mellow Fellow lineup in multiple formats. A smooth, sweet sativa with mild energizing effects — more approachable than Green Crack or Sour Diesel for those new to sativa effects.

Strawberry Amnesia — A sativa-dominant strain with bright fruity character and a euphoric effect profile. Look for it in the terp sauce and disposable categories.

Pineapple Express — Available in the Delta-8 Premium Disposable line. The Pineapple Express sativa-dominant strain delivers a tropical, uplifting effect that works particularly well in Delta-8 format — energizing without being overwhelming.

Lemon Tree — Featured in the Connection Blend Terp Sauce Spinner. Citrus-forward and social-leaning, Lemon Tree is a good choice for the Connection blend's mood-elevating cannabinoid profile.

Mandarin Cookies — In the Limitless Blend Terp Sauce Spinner. A bright, sweet sativa that pairs well with daytime use.

Power Plant — Featured in the Charged Blend Live Resin Disposable. Power Plant is a classic South African sativa known for energizing, clear-headed effects — a natural fit for the Charged blend.

Indica Strains in the Mellow Fellow Lineup

Granddaddy Purple (GDP) — Available in the Dream Blend Live Resin Disposable. GDP is one of the most well-known indica strains for a reason — it delivers deep body relaxation, mood softening, and significant sedation. The Dream blend is the right home for it.

Northern Lights — Featured in the THCp 0.5g Disposable line. Northern Lights is a pure indica known for full-body relaxation and strong sedation — paired with THCp's potency, this is a heavyweight nighttime combination for experienced users only.

Gas Mask — Available in the THCa Flower Sachet. A pungent, fuel-forward indica ideal for deep relaxation and end-of-day unwinding.

God's Gift — In the Introvert Premium 2ml Cart. Named for the deeply relaxing, introspective effect it produces — well-matched to the Introvert blend's calming cannabinoid formula.

Pure Kush — In the Wanderlust Terp Sauce 2ml Syringe. A classic heavy indica with earthy, kushy character and pronounced body sedation.

Green Goddess — Featured in the Tranquility Terp Sauce 2ml Cart. A relaxing, body-forward indica choice for the Tranquility blend.

Rose Gold Runtz — In the Laughter Blend Terp Sauce 2ml Syringe. A sweeter indica-leaning hybrid that softens the experience slightly while maintaining good body effect.

Hybrid Strains in the Mellow Fellow Lineup

Blue Dream — A sativa-dominant hybrid that appears across multiple Mellow Fellow formats. Blue Dream is known for balanced cerebral stimulation and mild body relaxation — genuinely versatile for both daytime and evening use. One of the most requested strains from Mellow Fellow customers.

Gary Payton — In the THCh 0.5g Disposable. A balanced hybrid known for a complex terpene profile with fuel and cookie notes. With THCh's potency, Gary Payton produces a well-rounded but intensely strong experience.

Jealousy — In the Euphoria Blend Terp Sauce Spinner. A balanced hybrid that rounds out the Euphoria blend's uplifting effect with mild grounding.

Wedding Cake — In the Delta-8 1ml Cart. A sweet, balanced hybrid with vanilla and earth notes. In Delta-8 format, it delivers a smooth, relaxed body effect.

Mimosa — In the Desire Premium 2ml Cart. A citrusy, uplifting hybrid — one of the more social and mood-forward options in the cart lineup.

Phantom OG — In the Dream Premium 2ml Cart. A hybrid leaning toward the relaxing side of the spectrum, good for unwinding without full sedation.

Permanent Marker — In the Wanderlust Blend 0.5ml Terp Sauce Disposable. A complex hybrid known for a striking terpene profile and balanced effect.

Mellow Fellow THCa Flower Strains

The THCa flower lineup carries its own distinct strain roster across jars, prerolls, sachets, blunts, and one-hitters. Highlights include Straight As Haze and Blue Waffles (sativa jars), Gas Mask (indica sachet), Shipwreck (sativa blunt), Stardawg (hybrid blunt), and several Private Reserve strains in the preroll collection including MF Reserve, Nightshade, Royal Kush, and Blue Lobster. The full flower range is in our flower section.

How to Choose the Right Mellow Fellow Strain

For daytime energy and focus: Green Crack, Sour Diesel, Power Plant, Jack Herer, or Lemon Tree. For social and uplifting experiences: Strawberry Cough, Blue Dream, Mimosa, or Jealousy. For creative sessions: Sour Diesel (Creativity Blend), Mandarin Cookies, or Strawberry Amnesia. For evening relaxation: Granddaddy Purple, God's Gift, Green Goddess, or Pure Kush. For maximum sedation (experienced users only): Northern Lights with THCp.

Frequently Asked Questions

What strains does Mellow Fellow carry?

Mellow Fellow carries Blue Dream, Green Crack, Sour Diesel, Granddaddy Purple, Northern Lights, Pineapple Express, Strawberry Cough, Gary Payton, Jack Herer, Wedding Cake, Mimosa, and many more across their vape, cart, and flower lineup.

Is Mellow Fellow Blue Dream indica or sativa?

Blue Dream is a sativa-dominant hybrid known for balanced cerebral stimulation with mild body relaxation. It is one of the most versatile strains in the Mellow Fellow lineup.

What is the best Mellow Fellow sativa?

Green Crack (Euphoria Blend Live Resin) and Sour Diesel (Creativity Blend) are the most energizing sativa options. Strawberry Cough is a gentler, more approachable sativa for those new to the effect.

What is the best Mellow Fellow indica?

Granddaddy Purple (Dream Blend) and Northern Lights (THCp Disposable) are the strongest indica experiences. God's Gift (Introvert Cart) and Green Goddess (Tranquility Cart) are more moderate options.

Does strain selection affect the high on Mellow Fellow products?

Yes, particularly on live resin and terp sauce products where terpene profiles are preserved. Sativa strains trend energizing, indicas trend relaxing, and hybrids balance both. The difference is most noticeable in high-quality formats like the live resin and Destination Series lines.
