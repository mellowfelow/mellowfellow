Mellow Fellow Delta-8 Review 2026: Every Product Tested

Mellow Fellow Delta-8: What to Expect

Delta-8 THC is a naturally occurring cannabinoid that delivers a smoother, milder high than Delta-9 — about half the potency, with less anxiety and more clarity. Mellow Fellow's Delta-8 lineup covers carts and disposables across a wide range of strains, making it one of the most accessible parts of the catalog for newer consumers or anyone who wants a lighter, more functional experience. If you have read our Delta-8 vs Delta-9 vs THCp breakdown, this is the deep-dive on the Delta-8 side specifically.

Delta-8 Vape Cartridges: Best Options

The Delta-8 1ml vape cartridges come in four strains: AK-47, Green Crack, Pink Runtz, and Wedding Cake. AK-47 is a classic sativa-leaning option with a earthy, woody flavor and a clear-headed, energizing effect — one of the best daytime Delta-8 options in the lineup. Green Crack is the most stimulating of the four — light on body, strong on mental energy and focus. Pink Runtz leans hybrid, with a sweet berry flavor and balanced effect. Wedding Cake is the most indica of the four — sweet, creamy, and relaxing. All four are available at the standard 1ml cart size. Shop Delta-8 carts.

Delta-8 Disposable Vapes: Full Lineup

The Delta-8 Premium 2ml disposable lineup is the largest Delta-8 format in the catalog — seven strains: Blueberry OG, Green Crack, Pineapple Express, Pink Runtz, Purple Punch, Strawberry Cough, and The Original Z. Strawberry Cough is the standout for flavor — bright, sweet, and one of the most pleasant-smelling vapes in the entire Mellow Fellow range. Pineapple Express is the best for daytime energy. Blueberry OG and Purple Punch are the most indica-leaning, ideal for evenings. The 2ml format gives you significantly more oil than a 0.5ml or 1ml — expect 300 to 400 puffs from a full 2ml disposable. Shop Delta-8 disposables.

Mellow Fellow Delta-8 vs HHC

HHC (hexahydrocannabinol) appears in several Mellow Fellow blend formulations alongside Delta-8. HHC sits closer to Delta-9 in potency than Delta-8 does — it is estimated at around 70-80% of Delta-9's strength compared to Delta-8's roughly 50%. In the blended products, HHC adds depth to the effect that straight Delta-8 cannot achieve on its own. If you have tried the pure Delta-8 line and want more potency without jumping straight to THCp, the Euphoria or Desire blend products — which combine Delta-8, HHC, and THCp — are the logical next step.

Who Should Start with Delta-8

Delta-8 is the right starting point if you are new to hemp-derived cannabinoids, sensitive to anxiety with stronger products, or want a functional daytime experience that does not impair concentration. The pure Delta-8 carts and disposables have the most predictable, gentle effect profile in the lineup.
