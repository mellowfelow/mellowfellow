Vape Cartridges vs Disposable Pens: What's the Difference?

A Mellow Fellow cartridge is a 510-thread cart that screws onto a separate rechargeable battery you already own. A Mellow Fellow disposable pen is an all-in-one device with the battery, cart, and oil sealed into one unit that you use and dispose of. Carts are more economical if you already have a battery and vape often; disposables are simpler and better for occasional use or travel. What Battery Works With Mellow Fellow Carts?

Any standard 510-thread vape battery is compatible with Mellow Fellow cartridges.
