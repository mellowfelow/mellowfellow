Mellow Fellow 2g vs 4ml vs 4g: Which Size Is Right for You?

Browsing Mellow Fellow products can get confusing when you start seeing 0.5g, 1ml, 2g, 2ml, 4ml, and 4g on different listings. This guide decodes every size format in the Mellow Fellow lineup — what each one contains, how long it lasts, and which size offers the best value for your usage pattern.

Understanding Mellow Fellow Product Sizes

Mellow Fellow uses different units for different product types, which is the main source of confusion. Here is the breakdown:

Disposable vapes are measured in grams (g) or milliliters (ml) of oil — these are effectively the same thing since cannabis oil has a density close to 1g/ml. A 2g disposable and a 2ml disposable contain the same volume of oil.

Vape cartridges are measured in milliliters (ml). A 0.5ml cart is a half-gram cart. A 2ml cart is a two-gram cart.

Flower products are measured in grams (g) of dried flower. A 4g flower sachet contains 4 grams of THCa flower — entirely different from a 4ml disposable.

The Full Size Range — Disposables

0.5g / 0.5ml — Entry size: The smallest Mellow Fellow disposable format. Found in the THCp disposable line and the THCh disposable line. A 0.5g disposable yields approximately 100–200 puffs depending on draw length. Good for trying a cannabinoid type or strain before committing to a larger size.

1ml — Mid-size disposable: The mid-tier. Found in the Wellness CBD line, the Wanderlust Blend, and several terp sauce spinner formats. Approximately 200–400 puffs. A solid one-to-two-week supply for regular use.

2ml / 2g — Most common format: This is the most prevalent size across the Mellow Fellow lineup — found in the live resin disposables, premium blend disposables, terp sauce carts, and premium carts. 400–600+ puffs is typical. This is the standard size most regular Mellow Fellow users default to. Offers significantly better value per ml than the 0.5g option.

4ml — Largest disposable format: The Euphoria Blend Live Resin Disposable and the Creativity Blend Live Resin Disposable are the flagship 4ml products. At 4ml, you are looking at 800–1,200+ puffs — a genuine long-term supply. The per-ml cost on 4ml products typically represents the best value in the vape lineup.

The Full Size Range — Cartridges

0.5ml carts: The THCp cartridge (Juice Box) comes in 0.5ml. Small, focused on potency, designed for consumers who want the most concentrated THCp experience in a cart format.

2ml carts: The majority of Mellow Fellow's vape cartridge lineup is in the 2ml format — Dream, Introvert, Tranquility, Desire, Charged, Clarity live resin, and the terp sauce variants. All require a compatible 510-thread battery. At 2ml, these carts are substantial and last well for regular users.

The Full Size Range — Flower

1g prerolls and one-hitters: Single-serving flower formats. One-hitters are small glass pipes pre-packed with 1g of THCa flower. Prerolls are 1g joints.

2g blunts: The 2g THCa blunt line features premium tobacco-free wraps with 2g of flower — larger than a standard preroll and designed to be shared or saved across multiple sessions.

3.5g flower jars: The THCa flower jar line is sold in 3.5g (eighth) portions — the standard unit of measure for dried flower.

4g sachets: The THCa flower sachets come in 4g sealed packs — slightly larger than a standard eighth, with a resealable format for freshness. Not to be confused with a 4ml vape product.

Which Size Should You Buy?

If you are trying Mellow Fellow for the first time: start with a 0.5g or 1ml disposable of whatever cannabinoid type interests you. Lower commitment, still gives you a genuine experience to evaluate. If you already know you like Mellow Fellow: the 2ml disposable or 2ml cart is the sweet spot — substantial supply, good value, the most options in terms of strains and blends.
