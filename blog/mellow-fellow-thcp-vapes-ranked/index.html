Mellow Fellow THCp Vapes Ranked 2026: Every Model Reviewed

Why THCp Is Different from Everything Else

THCp (tetrahydrocannabiphorol) binds to CB1 receptors with significantly higher affinity than Delta-9 THC — making it the most potent cannabinoid in the Mellow Fellow lineup by a wide margin. The 0.5ml THCp disposables in the Mellow Fellow catalog are not beginner products. They are for experienced consumers who want the strongest hemp-derived experience available. If you are new to THCp, start with our complete THCp explainer before ordering.

THCp 0.5g Disposables: Full Strain Lineup Ranked

Mellow Fellow's 0.5g THCp disposables come in ten strains. Here is how they rank. Blue Dream (sativa) — the most balanced THCp option in the lineup; euphoric, creative, not overwhelming. The best starting point for THCp newcomers. Northern Lights (indica) — classic deep sedation; one of the heaviest-hitting vapes in the entire catalog. For experienced consumers only. Pineapple Express (hybrid) — tropical flavor, uplifting effect, one of the most popular strains in the range. Strawberry Cough (sativa) — bright and energetic with a distinctive strawberry finish. Green Crack (sativa) — the most stimulating THCp option; energizing and mentally sharp. Granddaddy Purple (indica) — sweet grape, deeply relaxing, evening-only. White Runtz (hybrid) — creamy, balanced, one of the better-flavored options. Apple Mintz, Banana Pie, Forbidden Fruit — all strong indica-leaning options with dessert flavor profiles. THCp Cartridges: The B and T Series

The THCp 0.5ml vape cartridges use the B and T naming system — product codes that correspond to specific cannabinoid blend formulations. B-007, B-009, B-012 are part of the B series (blend-focused). T-006, T-010 are part of the T series (terp-sauce-style formulations with higher terpene content). B-012 is the highest-volume search term in this category and one of the most sought-after carts — it uses a potent multi-cannabinoid blend that pairs THCp with supporting cannabinoids for a complex, layered effect. T-010 is the most terpene-forward option, delivering stronger flavor alongside the potency. All cart options: Blueberry Muffins, Cereal Milk, Darkstar, Juice Box, Miami Haze, TKO. Shop THCp cartridges.

THCp Flower: Hash Holes and Infused Blunts

Beyond vapes, Mellow Fellow offers THCp in flower form — 2g THCp-infused blunts in six strains (Cereal Milk, Ice Cream Mintz, Kali Mist, Mellow Kush, Super Boof, Yoda OG) and 2g THCp hash hole prerolls (Diamond Daze sativa, Eclipse Kush indica, Golden Fellow hybrid). The hash hole format — a concentrate core running through the center of the preroll — delivers a slower, more even burn and a more concentrated hit. For infused flower one-hitters, four options are available: Lava Cake, Purple Goo, Rainbow Sherbet, and Vanilla Kush. Shop THCp prerolls.

THCp Dosing: Start Low

A single short pull on a THCp disposable is enough to assess the effect — wait 10-15 minutes before taking a second. The 0.5ml format is smaller than standard 1ml or 2ml vapes by design.
