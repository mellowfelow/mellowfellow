What Is THCh? The Rare Cannabinoid in Mellow Fellow's Most Potent Vapes

THCh — short for tetrahydrocannabihexol — is one of the newest rare cannabinoids to reach the hemp market, and it is quietly becoming the preferred choice for experienced consumers who want potency that sits between Delta-9 THC and the intensely powerful THCp. If you have seen THCh listed on a Mellow Fellow product and wondered exactly what it is, this guide covers everything: the science, the effects, how it compares to other cannabinoids, and what to expect if you decide to try it.

What Is THCh? The Short Answer

THCh (tetrahydrocannabihexol) is a naturally occurring phytocannabinoid found in trace amounts in the cannabis and hemp plant. It was first formally identified alongside THCp in a landmark 2019 study published in Scientific Reports. Like THCp, THCh has a longer alkyl side chain than standard Delta-9 THC — six carbons versus Delta-9's five — which gives it meaningfully stronger binding affinity for the body's CB1 receptors. The result is a cannabinoid that is noticeably more potent than Delta-9 but generally considered more manageable than THCp, making it an interesting middle ground for seasoned hemp users.

THCh vs Delta-9 THC vs THCp: How Do They Compare?

Understanding THCh is easier when you place it in context alongside the cannabinoids you may already know. The key difference between these three compounds comes down to their alkyl side chain length, which directly affects how strongly each one binds to CB1 receptors in the brain and nervous system.

Delta-9 THC has five carbons on its side chain and is the benchmark most people use. THCh has six carbons and is estimated to bind to CB1 receptors with roughly 10x the affinity of Delta-9 THC in early research models. THCp, with seven carbons, is estimated at up to 33x the CB1 affinity. In practical terms, this means THCh tends to produce stronger, longer-lasting effects than Delta-9 while still being more approachable than THCp — though all three are psychoactive and all require respect around dosing.

If you are already comfortable with Mellow Fellow THCp products, THCh will feel somewhat familiar. If you are coming from standard Delta-9 or Delta-8, treat THCh as a significant step up in potency.

What Does THCh Feel Like? Effects to Expect

User reports and early anecdotal evidence consistently describe THCh effects as deeply physical, with clear cerebral components as well. Expect pronounced body relaxation — more so than most Delta-9 experiences — alongside mood elevation, heightened sensory awareness, and an extended duration that typically runs longer than an equivalent Delta-9 session. Many users describe a warm, heavy body feeling paired with a calm mental clarity, though at higher doses the sedation becomes more dominant.

Because of its potency, THCh is best approached in the evening or during dedicated rest time, particularly if you are new to high-potency hemp cannabinoids. It is not a product for the middle of a workday, and it is absolutely not suitable for consumers who are inexperienced with cannabis or hemp products generally.

Hemp-derived THCh falls under the 2018 Farm Bill framework in the United States, which legalized hemp and hemp-derived cannabinoids at the federal level, provided the final product contains no more than 0.3% Delta-9 THC by dry weight.
