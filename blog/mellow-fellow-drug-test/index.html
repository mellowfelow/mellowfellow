Does Mellow Fellow Show Up on a Drug Test? What You Need to Know

If you use Mellow Fellow products and have a drug test coming up, this is the guide you need to read before your test — not after. The straightforward answer is that yes, Mellow Fellow products can and likely will trigger a positive result on a standard drug screen. Here is why, and what you need to know.

How Drug Tests Work

Standard urine drug tests — the most common type used by employers — do not test for THC itself. They test for THC-COOH, a metabolite your body produces when it processes any form of THC, including Delta-8, Delta-9, THCp, THCh, and THCa (once it converts to THC when heated). The test cannot distinguish between metabolites from a hemp product and metabolites from conventional cannabis. A metabolite is a metabolite.

The standard cutoff for a positive urine test is 50 nanograms per milliliter of THC-COOH. Depending on how frequently you use Mellow Fellow products and which ones, you can easily exceed this threshold.

Which Mellow Fellow Products Are Most Likely to Cause a Positive?

Virtually all Mellow Fellow products except the Wellness CBD line contain THC-producing cannabinoids. The higher the potency and the more frequently you use the product, the more metabolites will accumulate:

Highest risk: THCp products, THCh products, live resin disposables and carts, Delta-9 gummies, Max Dose edibles. These contain high concentrations of potent cannabinoids and regular use will almost certainly produce a positive test.

Moderate risk: Delta-8 products, terp sauce vapes, premium blend disposables. Delta-8 is technically a different compound from Delta-9, but it produces metabolites that standard tests still flag as a positive.

Lower (but not zero) risk: The Wellness CBD line. These products are designed to be low-THC, but trace amounts of THC can still accumulate with regular use. If drug testing is a genuine concern, even the wellness line carries some risk.

How Long Does Mellow Fellow Stay in Your System?

THC metabolites are fat-soluble, meaning they store in fat cells and are released gradually over time. Detection windows vary significantly:

Occasional use (once or twice): 3–5 days in urine.

Regular use (several times per week): 10–21 days.

Daily or heavy use: 30 days or more.

Blood tests have shorter windows (hours to a few days). Hair follicle tests have the longest window — up to 90 days. Saliva tests detect very recent use only (hours).

Body fat percentage, metabolism, hydration, and exercise all affect how quickly metabolites clear. There is no reliable shortcut to accelerating clearance.

Are Mellow Fellow Products Safe? (Lab Testing)

This question often comes up alongside the drug test question, and the answer is a clear yes — from a product safety and purity standpoint. Mellow Fellow third-party tests every product for cannabinoid potency, pesticides, heavy metals, and residual solvents. All products are farm-bill compliant hemp. The question of whether they show on a drug test is entirely separate from whether the products are safe to consume.

What to Do Before a Drug Test

There are no guaranteed methods to clear your system faster, despite what some products claim. The only reliable approach is time. If you have a test scheduled:

Stop using all Mellow Fellow products immediately. Allow as much time as possible before the test. Stay hydrated and maintain normal physical activity. Do not attempt to dilute your sample on the day of the test — labs screen for dilution and it can raise red flags of its own.

If you are subject to regular drug testing for employment, healthcare, or legal reasons, Mellow Fellow products are not the right choice for you, regardless of their hemp-derived legal status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Mellow Fellow show up on a drug test?

Yes, it can. Hemp-derived cannabinoids including Delta-8, Delta-9, THCp, and THCa all produce THC metabolites that standard drug tests detect. There is no guarantee any Mellow Fellow product will result in a clean test.

How long does Mellow Fellow stay in your system?

THC metabolites can remain detectable in urine for 3–30 days depending on frequency of use, metabolism, body fat percentage, and hydration. Daily users may test positive for longer.

Are Mellow Fellow carts safe to use?

Yes, from a product quality perspective. All Mellow Fellow products are third-party lab tested for potency, pesticides, heavy metals, and residual solvents. COAs are available for every batch.

Will Mellow Fellow Wellness CBD fail a drug test?

The Wellness CBD line carries lower risk but is not zero risk. Even broad-spectrum CBD can contain trace THC that accumulates with regular use. If drug testing is a firm concern, avoid all cannabinoid products.

What should I do if I have a drug test coming up?

Stop using all hemp and cannabis products as soon as possible and allow adequate time. No supplement or drink can guarantee a clean result. Consult your employer or testing agency if you have specific concerns.
