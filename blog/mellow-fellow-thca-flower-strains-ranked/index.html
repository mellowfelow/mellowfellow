Mellow Fellow THCa Flower Strains Ranked 2026: Complete Exotic Guide

Mellow Fellow THCa Flower: What You Are Getting

THCa (tetrahydrocannabinolic acid) is the raw, unheated form of THC found in the cannabis plant. In flower form, THCa is non-psychoactive at room temperature — but when you apply heat (smoking, vaporizing), it converts to Delta-9 THC in real time through a process called decarboxylation. This means Mellow Fellow THCa flower delivers a full-strength, full-spectrum cannabis experience while remaining compliant under hemp law. For the science behind this, see our THCa vs THC explainer. Here we focus on the strains themselves — what each one is, what it feels like, and which ones are worth your order.

3.5g Exotic Flower Jars: Top Picks

The 3.5g jar is the standard eighth — the most popular format for flower. Mellow Fellow carries twelve strains in this size. Blue Waffles (sativa) — one of the most unique flavor profiles in the lineup; sweet, fruity, and surprisingly smooth. Great daytime strain. Straight A's Haze (sativa) — clear-headed, energetic, classic haze character with citrus notes. Strong THCa percentage. Deep Ztate (hybrid) — balanced, complex, good for creative work or social situations. Granddaddy Kush (hybrid) — one of the most relaxing hybrid strains in the lineup; OG-style earthiness and pine. Frosty OG (indica) — heavy, classic indica experience with dense trichome coverage. California Gold (indica) — smooth, golden-hued, deeply relaxing. For one-hitter format options in exotic strains, the Blueberry Caviar, Permanent Marker, Sour Haze, Space Kush, Triple Cheese, and Zookies one-hitters offer the same quality at a lower commitment per strain. Shop 3.5g flower jars.

1oz THCa Flower Jars: Best Value Bulk

The 1oz exotic flower jars — Apricot Haze (sativa), Grape Cream Cake (hybrid), Mac Daddy (indica), Trop Cherry (indica), Willy Wonka (hybrid), Z-Pie (sativa) — offer the best per-gram value in the flower catalog. Apricot Haze is the most distinctive and unique of the six: bright apricot aroma, energizing sativa effect, and exceptionally smooth smoke. Z-Pie is the most potent sativa in the 1oz range. Mac Daddy is the heaviest indica, with a classic OG-style sedating effect. Willy Wonka hybrid strikes the best balance if you want one all-day jar. Shop 1oz flower jars.

THCa Prerolls and Blunts

If grinding and rolling is not your format, Mellow Fellow's preroll and blunt lineup covers every style. The 1g prerolls come in AK Reserve (sativa) and Luxury Leaf (hybrid) — solid daily-driver options. The Private Reserve 2-piece packs cover Blue Lobster (hybrid), MF Reserve (indica), Nightshade (indica), Royal Kush (hybrid), and Sour Haze (sativa) — the Private Reserve designation signals higher-tier flower with above-average THCa percentages. The 2g THCa exotic blunts are the premium format: Jealousy, Jelly Roll, Mr. Nasty, Shipwreck, Stardawg, and Sweet Cheese. Stardawg (hybrid) and Shipwreck (sativa) are the most popular. The 2g hash hole prerolls — Daydream (sativa), Grape Ape OG (indica), Space Cake (hybrid) — are the top of the flower lineup: a concentrate core inside the preroll for a slower burn and significantly elevated potency. Shop THCa prerolls.

THCa Sachets: Try Before You Commit

The 1g THCa sachets are the lowest-commitment way to try exotic strains — six options: Banana Sundae (sativa), Garlic Mintz (indica), Gary Payton (hybrid), Gas Mask (indica), Pink Runtz (hybrid), Purple Champagne (sativa). The 6-pack sampler is the smartest buy for new customers — one gram of six different strains to find your favorites before ordering in larger quantities. Gary Payton hybrid is consistently the highest-rated of the six on effect and flavor. Shop THCa sachets.

THCa Budder Concentrates

For concentrate users, the THCa Budder Jar lineup extends the flower catalog into extraction territory: six strains in 2g jars — Bandz (sativa), Garlic Cookies (indica), Kush Cake (sativa), Peach Oblivion x Soul Cleanser (hybrid), Reba Jam (hybrid), Sour Orange Apricot (indica). THCa budder is a whipped concentrate with a soft, creamy texture that is easy to work with. Kush Cake and Garlic Cookies are the most popular for potency. Shop THCa budder jars.

How to Choose Your Strain

Start with the effect you want: energy and creativity → sativa (Straight A's Haze, Apricot Haze, Z-Pie, Sour Haze). Balanced all-day → hybrid (Granddaddy Kush, Deep Ztate, Gary Payton, Willy Wonka). Evening and sleep → indica (Frosty OG, Mac Daddy, California Gold, Northern Lights). If you are new to THCa flower, see our THCa flower beginner's guide. Shop all Mellow Fellow flower.
