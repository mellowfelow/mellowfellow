Mellow Fellow Destination Series: Complete Flavor Guide

Mellow Fellow Destination Series: Complete Flavor and Blend Guide

The Destination Series is Mellow Fellow's flagship premium line — city-inspired multi-cannabinoid blends in both 2mL and 4mL dual-chamber formats. Each blend is formulated around a specific effect profile named after an inspiration city. Here is the complete breakdown of every blend in the current lineup.

Sativa-Leaning Blends

Creativity (Nashville) — Sour Diesel, Terp Sauce: Delta-8, HHC, THCp, CBD, CBG. The most energizing blend in the lineup. Sour Diesel terpenes with a THCp edge make this a genuinely productive daytime option. Community consensus: best sativa in the Destination Series. Best for creative work, exercise, or social settings.

Limitless (Cape Town) — Durban Poison: Delta-8, THCV, HHC, CBD. The THCV addition sets this apart — a rare cannabinoid associated with appetite modulation and energetic focus. Durban Poison terpenes are sharp and bright. Best for daytime active use.

Euphoria (Ibiza) — Strawberry Amnesia, Live Resin: Delta-8, HHC, THCV, CBD. One of the best-smelling vapes in the catalog — the live resin terpene quality is exceptional here. Social and uplifting.

Hybrid Blends

Clarity (Amsterdam) — Jack Herer: THCV, HHC, Delta-8, CBD, CBG. Jack Herer focus combined with THCV produces unusual mental clarity — less of a high, more of clean sharp alertness.

Desire (Maui) — Maui Wowie: Delta-8, HHC, THCp, CBD. Tropical, balanced, and approachable. A great all-rounder for those not sure which direction to go.

Tranquility (Lake Tahoe) — Tahoe OG / Strawberry Amnesia, 4mL Duo: HHC, CBD, CBN, H4CBD. The dual-chamber design lets you switch between strains or blend them mid-session. Best hardware innovation in the lineup.

Indica-Leaning Blends

Dream (London) — Royal Runtz: Delta-8, CBN, THCp, HHC, CBD. CBN makes this the most sedating blend — specifically formulated for evening use and sleep support. Most consistently recommended blend for nighttime relaxation.

Introvert (Tokyo) — Ice Cream Cake / Grape Ape, 4mL Duo: HHC, Delta-8, THCp, CBD, CBN. The heaviest indica in the Destination Series. For quiet evenings in.

Which Should You Start With?

New to Destination Series: start with Desire (balanced) or Euphoria (uplifting, social). Most potent option: Dream or Introvert. Best for productivity: Clarity or Creativity. Browse the full Destination Series collection here.
