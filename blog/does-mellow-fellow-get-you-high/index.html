Does Mellow Fellow Get You High? Honest Effects Guide 2026

Mellow Fellow makes hemp-derived products — and a common question from first-time customers is a simple one: does Mellow Fellow actually get you high? The short answer is yes, often significantly so. The longer answer depends on which product you choose, your individual tolerance, and how you use it. This guide breaks it all down.

What Makes Mellow Fellow Products Psychoactive?

Mellow Fellow products contain hemp-derived cannabinoids that interact with the body's endocannabinoid system — specifically the CB1 receptors in the brain and nervous system. The cannabinoids in the lineup include Delta-8 THC, Delta-9 THC, THCp, THCh, THCa, and various blends of these compounds. All of them are psychoactive to varying degrees. None of them are CBD-only products.

The brand does carry a Wellness CBD line, which is non-intoxicating — but if you are shopping in the vapes, cartridges, edibles, or flower categories, you are buying products that will produce noticeable effects.

Which Mellow Fellow Products Are the Strongest?

Potency varies dramatically across the catalog. Here is a practical breakdown from mildest to most intense:

Mild: Delta-8 products, Wellness CBD, Microdose edibles (80mg). Good entry points for new users or those with low tolerance.

Moderate: Live Resin disposables and cartridges, Premium Blend disposables, Delta-9 gummies. These deliver clear, enjoyable psychoactive effects for most experienced users.

Strong: THCp disposables and THCp cartridges. THCp binds to CB1 receptors an estimated 33x more strongly than Delta-9 THC. This is not a beginner cannabinoid.

Very Strong: THCh disposables and the Max Dose edible line. THCh sits between Delta-9 and THCp in potency. Max Dose edibles push total cannabinoid content to 10,000mg per package — for very experienced consumers only.

What Does the High Feel Like? Effects by Product Type

Delta-8 vapes and carts: Smooth, relaxed, slightly sedating. Noticeably milder than Delta-9 with less anxiety. Good for daytime or wind-down sessions.

Live Resin vapes: Fuller, more nuanced effect thanks to preserved terpene profiles. The live resin disposables tend to produce a well-rounded high that reflects the underlying strain character.

Destination Series terp sauce spinners: Effect varies by blend — Euphoria leans uplifting and social, Dream is deeply relaxing, Creativity is energizing. Read the complete Destination Series guide to choose by intended effect.

THCp products: Intense, fast-acting, long-lasting. One or two draws can be more than enough. Users describe a powerful body effect alongside strong cerebral involvement. Not suitable for casual or first-time use.

Gummies and edibles: Delayed onset (30–90 minutes), longer duration (4–8 hours). The THCp Bursts at 25mg each are potent. The Microdose line at 80mg total is the gentler option.

THCa flower: THCa is non-psychoactive in its raw form, but converts to THC when heated — meaning Mellow Fellow THCa flower, prerolls, blunts and one-hitters produce full THC effects when smoked or vaped. For more, see our THCa flower explainer.

Does Mellow Fellow Have Good Lab Testing?

Yes. Every Mellow Fellow product is third-party lab tested and certificates of analysis (COAs) are available. This matters because it means the potency levels listed on labels are verified — you are getting what you paid for. For a deep dive on what to look for in a COA and why it matters, read our full safety and lab testing guide.

How to Dial In Your Experience

Start lower than you think you need to, especially on your first order. If you are new to hemp cannabinoids entirely, the beginner product guide walks you through the gentlest entry points. If you have prior cannabis experience and want something that delivers, live resin or a terp sauce blend are reliable choices. If you are a seasoned consumer seeking maximum potency, THCp or THCh is where Mellow Fellow delivers its most powerful experiences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Mellow Fellow get you high?

Yes. Mellow Fellow products contain psychoactive cannabinoids including THCp, Delta-8, Delta-9, THCh, and THCa, all of which produce intoxicating effects when used as directed. Potency varies significantly by product.

Which Mellow Fellow product is the strongest?

THCp and THCh products are the most potent in the lineup. THCp is estimated to bind CB1 receptors up to 33x more strongly than Delta-9 THC. Max Dose edibles are the strongest edible option.

Is Mellow Fellow a good brand?

Yes. Mellow Fellow is a well-regarded hemp brand with a wide product range, consistent third-party testing, and a loyal customer base. All products are farm-bill compliant.

How long does a Mellow Fellow high last?

Vapes typically produce effects lasting 1–3 hours. Edibles take 30–90 minutes to onset but can last 4–8 hours depending on dose and individual tolerance.

Is it possible to feel nothing from Mellow Fellow?

If you feel little effect, you may have started below your tolerance threshold or taken too small a dose from the vape. Try a slightly longer draw and wait at least 15 minutes before re-dosing. If you routinely use cannabis, consider moving up to a live resin or THCp product.
