Mellow Fellow Wellness Gummies Guide 2026: Every Product Explained

What Makes Mellow Fellow Wellness Gummies Different

The Mellow Fellow wellness line is built around non-psychoactive and low-psychoactive cannabinoids — CBD, CBG, CBN, THCV, CBDV, H4CBD, and CBC — formulated into pharmacist-reviewed blends targeting specific outcomes: sleep, focus, energy, happiness, recovery, and relief. These are not standard CBD gummies. Each blend uses a multi-cannabinoid approach that takes advantage of the entourage effect — the way cannabinoids work better in combination than in isolation. If you want the effects without the high, this is where to start in the Mellow Fellow catalog.

Happy CBG Gummies: Best for Mood

CBG (cannabigerol) is sometimes called the mother cannabinoid — it is the precursor from which many other cannabinoids are synthesized, and it has strong evidence for mood-lifting and anti-anxiety effects without psychoactivity. The Happy CBG Gummies come in two sizes: 100mg (strawberry, 10mg per gummy) and 1500mg (strawberry and green apple, higher-strength for regular users). The 1500mg jars are the best value for daily CBG users. Strawberry is the better-reviewed flavor of the two. Best for: daily mood support, anxiety reduction, general wellness. Shop Happy CBG gummies.

Relief CBD Gummies: Best for Physical Comfort

The Relief CBD Gummies target physical discomfort — soreness, tension, inflammation recovery. Available in 100mg peach and 100mg watermelon (10mg CBD per gummy). Straightforward, clean CBD gummies with no psychoactive components. The watermelon flavor is the stronger seller; both are consistently reviewed positively for taste. These pair well with the Recover blend vapes for a full physical recovery routine. Shop Relief CBD gummies.

Sleep CBN Gummies: Best for Rest

CBN (cannabinol) is the cannabinoid most studied for sleep support — it has a mild sedative quality that helps with sleep onset without the heavy-handed effect of high-THCp products. The Sleep CBN Gummies (100mg, cherry, 10mg CBN per piece) are the best option in the catalog for people who want sleep support without any psychoactive experience. Cherry is the only flavor available in this line. Take 1-2 pieces 30-45 minutes before bed. Shop Sleep CBN gummies.

The Functional Blend Gummies (Tropic Medley Line)

Mellow Fellow also offers a more advanced functional gummy line under the Tropic Medley branding — specific blends targeting energy (THCV + CBG), focus (CBDV + CBG), recovery (CBC + CBD), and happiness (H4CBD). These use cannabinoids that most competitors do not work with. The Energy Blend 1500mg and Focus Blend 600mg are the standouts for daytime functional use. The Happy Blend 2000mg is the largest and most cost-effective for regular users who prioritize mood. The Burn Blend and Rest Blend round out the line for post-activity recovery and wind-down. Browse all functional gummies.

Wellness Gummies vs Psychoactive Gummies: How to Choose

If you want zero high at all, stick to the CBD, CBG, and CBN products. If you want a very mild psychoactive effect alongside the wellness benefits, the Microdose M-Fusions (10mg Delta-9 + CBD) are the bridge between the two worlds. For a complete overview of the psychoactive edibles side, see our full gummies lineup review. For the wellness products, the 5-Pack Sampler — two gummies each of five different wellness blends — is the best way to try the line without committing to a full jar. Shop the wellness sampler.
